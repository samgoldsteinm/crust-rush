Turning WebGPU on

  
iPhone and iPad on iOS 18: Settings app → Apps → Safari → Advanced → Feature Flags → turn on WebGPU, then reload this page. On iOS 26 it is already on.

  
Android (Chrome): on by default on most phones from Android 12 up; some budget phones have no WebGPU and will use WebGL.

  
Computers: Chrome and Edge have it on by default; Firefox has it on recent versions; Safari 26 on Mac has it on.
