Built for your graphics card

On Ultra the PC version matches the browser game's best look, then goes further with PC extras, on by default:

  • Shadows twice as far, in four cascades that soften with distance.
  • Bounced light between nearby surfaces, and ambient occlusion in the corners.
  • Finished scenery: spruces, palms, leafy bushes, real stone and a cheese ground with depth. Leaves sway in the wind.
  • Sharper textures at an angle, and glossy paint that reflects a finer sky.
  • Struggling? Turn PC extras off in Settings > Graphics, or pick Balanced.

Install

  1. Download CrustRush-Windows.zip (about 900 MB).
  2. Unzip it anywhere: right-click > Extract All. Keep the maps folder next to the game.
  3. Run CrustRush.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. The game isn't signed, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet.
  4. Updating: download again and replace the folder. Your records and settings are kept.
